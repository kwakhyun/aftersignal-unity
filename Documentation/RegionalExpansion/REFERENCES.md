# Regional architecture and road fleet — design references

Reviewed 2026-09-08. Vehicle meshes are original game designs, authored in Blender from proportions and recognizable design cues. They are not scanned replicas, downloaded manufacturer models, or official licensed vehicles. Editable sources: `Documentation/Mobility/Models/*.blend`; regeneration: `Tools/WorldExpansion/create_reference_fleet.py`.

Architecture is built from original parametric meshes and existing project surface materials. Reference photographs and diagrams were consulted, not redistributed as assets.

- [Foster + Partners — City Hall](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/city-hall/): stepped, displaced glazed civic volume; applied to the Nova assembly crown.
- [Snøhetta — Calgary Central Library](https://www.snohetta.com/projects/calgary-central-library): timber roof structure and angled glazing rhythm; applied to Nova's library.
- [Zaha Hadid Foundation — Vitra Fire Station](https://www.zhfoundation.com/collections/vitra-fire-station/): long angular dispatch canopy and exposed structural form; applied to Nova's rescue station.
- [OCEANIX](https://oceanix.com/media/): clustered maritime infrastructure and productive community spaces; applied to islands and Nereid's pressure district.

The lowland district, Erebos collapse, ruined institutions, pressure vessels, island shore meshes and furnished interiors are original fictional layouts. No claim of reproducing a real city's topology or structural engineering is made.
