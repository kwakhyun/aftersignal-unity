# Creature modeling references

The three delivered meshes are original procedural Blender work. No external creature mesh, image, logo, or texture is redistributed.

- Madeleine Spencer, creature artist, [Pacific Rim: Uprising Kaiju creature design](https://maddiemonster.artstation.com/projects/k4gqKx). Primary artist portfolio describing ZBrush design, skin variation studies, and high resolution creature texture work. Used as a production reference for separate silhouette, armour, and surface detail passes.
- Rebecca DarkEdgeTV, [Nemesyth Dragon Bust](https://rebecca1208.artstation.com/projects/k8oeK). Primary artist model showcase, referenced for long cranial proportions and horn/neck surface hierarchy.
- Blender Studio, [Anglerfish character creation](https://studio.blender.org/training/anglerfish/). Public training overview of creature design, retopology and rigging. The paid lesson content was not accessed.
- Blender, [Sculpting features](https://www.blender.org/features/sculpting/). Official modeling/sculpting workflow reference.

Runtime materials use the project's existing URP material foundation. Animated transforms retain separate thorax, cranium, tail and limb pivots. Blender source files are included for further manual sculpting and animation refinement.
