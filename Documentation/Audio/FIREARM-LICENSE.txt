AFTERSIGNAL uses edited excerpts of 1911, Walther PPQ, Bersa, Mossberg, AR-15 and AK-47 recordings. Processing includes onset trimming, mono channel selection, 48 kHz resampling, peak normalization and short edge fades. These creators do not endorse this game.
